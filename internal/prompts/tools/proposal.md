Settles retained sub-agent proposals produced by `delegate`, selected by `action`. `list` shows open proposals; `show` returns one proposal's diff and changed files by `proposal_id`. Two lanes produce them: a worktree proposal keeps a live isolated worktree until you settle it and merges three-way; a container proposal's world is destroyed at seal, so the proposal is the whole-path delta manifest under `.yaah/proposals/<proposal_id>/` (rehydrated on restart), settled by writing whole-path post-images with no merge. To land the work: `select` fast-forwards onto the dispatch basis and fails closed if the tree moved since the dispatch; `apply` is path-disjoint onto a moved tree — three-way on the worktree lane, whole-path post-images on the container lane — and is refused on overlap with host changes on either lane. To not land it: `release` exports the delta to a retention path and then tears the worktree down (worktree lane only — a container world already ended at seal); `discard` throws the proposal away and removes its worktree, or its manifest directory.
