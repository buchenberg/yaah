Settles retained sub-agent proposals produced by `delegate`, selected by `action`. `list` shows open proposals; `show` returns one proposal's diff and changed files by `proposal_id`. Two lanes produce them: a worktree proposal keeps a live isolated worktree until you settle it and merges three-way; a container proposal's world is destroyed at seal, so the proposal is the whole-path delta manifest under `.yaah/proposals/<proposal_id>/` (host-target rehydrated on restart; session-target is a session-lifetime audit artifact), settled by writing whole-path post-images with no merge. To land the work: `select` fast-forwards onto the dispatch basis and fails closed if that basis moved since the dispatch; `apply` is path-disjoint onto a moved tree — three-way on the worktree lane, whole-path post-images on the container lane — and is refused on overlap with parent changes on either lane. To not land it: `release` exports the delta to a retention path and then tears the worktree down (worktree lane only — a container world already ended at seal); `discard` throws the proposal away and removes its worktree, or its manifest directory.

The dispatch envelope carries `world` (`worktree` or `container`); a container dispatch or settlement envelope also carries `target` (`host` or `session`). An optional `warning` means the dispatch ran in a degraded world instead of the one requested — read it before deciding. An optional `notes` records a settlement caveat such as a failed teardown or stranded worktree. A session-target proposal settles into the session container; if `apply_out` is enabled in this session, use it to publish the accepted files to the host. The settlement verbs and the consume-once rule do not change.

Every response uses one envelope. `status` is `proposal` for a dispatch, `settled` after a verdict, `open` for `list`, or `error` with the reason in `error`. A dispatch adds `proposal_id`, the changed `files`, a `diff`, an optional `result`, and a `next` list of legal verbs; a settlement adds the `action`, an optional `retained` release path, and the rendered `outcome`. A `list` response carries `proposals` summaries, each with `proposal_id`, `files`, and `target`, plus `next`.
