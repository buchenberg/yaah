Settles retained sub-agent proposals produced by `delegate`, selected by `action`. `list` shows open proposals; `show` returns one proposal's diff and changed files by `proposal_id`. To land the work: `select` fast-forwards the proposal onto the working tree and fails closed if the tree moved since the dispatch; `apply` three-way merges onto a moved tree and is path-disjoint or refused. To not land it: `release` exports the delta to a retention path and then tears the worktree down; `discard` throws the proposal away and removes its worktree. An open proposal retains its isolated worktree until you settle it one way or the other.
