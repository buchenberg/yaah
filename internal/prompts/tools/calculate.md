Evaluates a mathematical expression and returns the result. Supports arithmetic (`+` `-` `*` `/` `%` `**`), bitwise (`&` `|` `^` `<<` `>>`), functions (sqrt, abs, ceil, floor, round, log, log10, log2, exp, sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, min, max), constants (`pi`, `e`), and literals in hex (`0xFF`), binary (`0b1010`), and octal (`0o777`). Use for quick computation instead of running a shell command.
